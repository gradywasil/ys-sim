# 005: The rest of the hazards, and four more power-ups

The art backlog is now all in the game: wind, traps, webs, a spider, a loose board and a dust cloud, plus the fan, tape, bubble wrap and gloves to deal with them.

## New hazards

- **Wind gusts** (Backyard onward): periodic gusts slow P1 to a crawl while it leans into them. A placed **fan** blows P1 forward and cancels the wind around it.
- **Mousetraps** and **dripping pipes** (Basement onward): cycling hazards like the burner. P1 waits out the snap or drip. **Tape** pins one down for good (the preview highlights the target), and the stopwatch freezes them (an umbrella also blocks drips).
- **Spider** (Basement onward): drops on a thread as P1 passes and scares it stiff for a moment. A **flashlight** or **gloves** shoos it away.
- **Cobwebs** (Attic): P1 runs into the web and gets stuck for a bit. **Gloves** tear straight through.
- **Loose boards** (Attic): look like floor, drop after P1 steps on one, and turn into a 96-unit pit. Tape pins one; boots or sugar can outrun it; afterwards the usual wide-gap answers apply.
- **Dust clouds** (Attic): a puff that fogs the whole screen, so you can't see what's coming.

## New power-ups

- **Bubble wrap**: absorbs one bad event (a hazard hit, a slip, a web, a scare) with a satisfying pop. It trips P1 if boots or sugar are active.
- **Gloves**: tear through webs and shoo spiders. They trip P1 if it's in mud or on a puddle.
- **Fan** and **tape** (placeables): where you drop them matters; a drop onto P1's head trips it.

That's 15 power-ups and 16 hazards and zones now, in a scrolling item bar. A headless bot plays P2 against hundreds of seeds (and forced-chunk runs of each new hazard) to check every level is still solvable.
