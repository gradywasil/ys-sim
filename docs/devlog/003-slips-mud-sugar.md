# 003: Slippery floors, mud, slippers and sugar rush

Floor patches that P1 can't see coming, so Player 2 has to spot them and act.

## What's new

- **Juice puddles** (Kitchen onward): P1 runs straight in, loses its feet and slides about 160 units out of control (no steering, no jumping), then gets up. A slide that ends in a gap means a fall, so some chunks put a puddle right before a pit.
- **Mud** (Backyard onward): P1 slogs through at about a third of its speed.
- **Slippers** (a buff): P1 grips the floor, so puddles don't make it slip and mud only slows it a little. They only go on a P1 that is *running on the ground*: hand them over while it's standing or in the air and P1 trips.
- **Sugar rush** (Basement onward): P1 runs 1.8x faster and refuses to stop for anything, which clears wide gaps and speeds through mud. It also means P1 charges straight into the next hazard, and crashes (slows down) when it wears off. Only worth it on a clear stretch.
- A slip costs bedtime, and so do the hurts from hazards, so being ready matters.
- The item bar scrolls now (8 slots at a time, `Q`/`E` or the wheel to move) because there are 11 power-ups.
- Bot sweeps still clear everything, and the bot now also leads a running P1 with slippers ahead of puddles (and avoids sugar when a hazard is close).
